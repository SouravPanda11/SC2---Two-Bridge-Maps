Experiments and Results

Experiment 1: Pilot

V2_Base
Unit Count:
F = E
Objective Proximity:
Combat = Beacon
Strategic Question:
Can PPO resolve the balanced V2_Base setup without collapsing into piecemeal combat or delayed navigation?
PPO | V2_Base | Combat Loss

Policy Behavior: The agent repeatedly sends single units toward the enemy using move commands. Upon entering enemy firing range, units take damage without retaliating, leading to isolated and unfavorable engagements. This piecemeal strategy continues until all units are lost, resulting in a combat loss.

PPO | V2_Base | Navigation Win

Policy Behavior: The agent sends a single unit to the right, then redirects it toward the upper region where it remains idle for a period. It later issues an attack command, causing the unit to move toward the enemy at the bottom. Along this path, the unit unintentionally enters the beacon capture radius, resulting in an incidental navigation win.
