Experiment 2: Action Masking

Experiment 2: Terminal outcome distribution and training dynamics for MaskPPO on all variants.

V1_Base
Unit Count:
F > E
Objective Proximity:
Combat = Beacon
Strategic Question:
How does numerical advantage bias the agent toward combat when objectives are equidistant?
MaskablePPO | V1_Base | Combat Win

Policy Behavior: The agent issues an attack command to all 5 friendly units. Midway, it divides them into two groups: one unit continues the initial attack, while the remaining 4 are moved to an out-of-map coordinate on the extreme right. When the single unit reaches enemy proximity, the 4 units are again issued an attack command. Due to sheer force, this results in a combat win.

MaskablePPO | V1_Base | Navigation Win

Policy Behavior: The agent issues a move command to all 5 friendly units toward the top-right. After they begin moving, it divides them into two groups: 3 units are given an attack command, while the other 2 continue toward the beacon. Before reaching the beacon, the agent assigns an attack command to 4 units (the original 3 plus 1 from the beacon group), and that unit starts moving toward the enemy. One of the units from the beacon group moves toward the enemies, encounters the beacon, and results in a navigation win. The combat group has not yet engaged in combat. The remaining beacon unit stays there, as it was only assigned a move command.

V1_Combat
Unit Count:
F > E
Objective Proximity:
Combat < Beacon
Strategic Question:
How does numerical advantage bias the agent toward proximal combat over distant navigation?
MaskablePPO | V1_Combat | Combat Win

Policy Behavior: Initially, the agent issues an attack command to all units. While units are in transit, it forms varying sub-groups (3 or 4 units) and assigns them to attack the same target. Once engagement begins, it commands all units to attack together. Due to sheer force, this results in a combat win.

V1_Navigate
Unit Count:
F > E
Objective Proximity:
Combat > Beacon
Strategic Question:
How does numerical advantage bias the agent toward distant combat despite proximal navigation?
MaskablePPO | V1_Navigate | Combat Win

Policy Behavior: Issues an attack command to all units. Units engage in combat and win due to sheer force.

MaskablePPO | V1_Navigate | Navigation Win

Policy Behavior: Issues an attack command to all units. During transit toward the enemies, if any unit comes close to the beacon capture radius, it results in an incidental navigation win.

V2_Base
Unit Count:
F = E
Objective Proximity:
Combat = Beacon
Strategic Question:
How does the agent arbitrate between equidistant navigation and stochastic combat objectives?
MaskablePPO | V2_Base | Combat Win

Policy Behavior: Sends all units to attack; due to the stochastic nature of combat, this results in a win.

MaskablePPO | V2_Base | Navigation Win

Policy Behavior: Initially sends all units to attack. After losing one unit, it changes strategy and sends all units toward the upper right. If the beacon is encountered along the path, it results in a navigation win.

V2_Combat
Unit Count:
F = E
Objective Proximity:
Combat < Beacon
Strategic Question:
How does the agent arbitrate between proximal combat and distant navigation under equal unit counts?
MaskablePPO | V2_Combat | Combat Win

Policy Behavior: Sends all units to attack. After losing 2 units, it disengages from combat and retreats toward the top-right by issuing move commands to the remaining 3 units. As enemies follow, it re-engages in combat and wins.

MaskablePPO | V2_Combat | Navigation Win

Policy Behavior: Sends all units to attack. After losing 3 units, it disengages from combat and issues a move command to an out-of-map coordinate in the upper-left region. The remaining units move toward the upper-left, where the beacon is located. Midway, the two units fragment—one appears to turn back while the other continues on the same path. The unit reaches the beacon, resulting in a navigation win.

V2_Navigate
Unit Count:
F = E
Objective Proximity:
Combat > Beacon
Strategic Question:
How does the agent arbitrate between proximal navigation and distant combat under equal unit counts?
MaskablePPO | V2_Navigate | Combat Win

Policy Behavior: Issues a move command to an out-of-map coordinate on the extreme right, moving toward the bottom. After some time, it issues an attack command and engages in combat on the other side of the bridge. The agent wins due to the stochastic nature of combat, without forming a proper formation.

Maskable PPO | V2_Navigate | Navigation Win

Policy Behavior: Sends all units to attack. After losing 1 unit, it disengages from combat and issues a move command to out-of-map coordinates toward the extreme right, moving upward. The move coordinates lie near the beacon, and the units enter the beacon capture radius, resulting in a navigation win.

Maskable PPO | V3_Base | Combat Loss

Objective Proximity:
Combat < Beacon
Strategic Question:
How does numerical disadvantage bias the agent away from proximal combat in favor of distant navigation?
MaskablePPO | V3_Combat | Combat Loss

Policy Behavior: Sends 4 units to attack and loses due to unfavorable unit counts. It then sends the last remaining unit to attack.

V3_Navigate
Unit Count:
F < E
Objective Proximity:
Combat > Beacon
Strategic Question:
How does numerical disadvantage reinforce commitment to proximal navigation over distant combat?
MaskablePPO | V3_Navigate | Navigation Win

Policy Behavior: Issues a move command over a short distance, moving downward. It then oscillates between attack and move commands toward the bottom. Along this path, it incidentally encounters the beacon and wins.
