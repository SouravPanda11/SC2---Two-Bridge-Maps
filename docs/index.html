Scaling Strategy, Not Compute: A Stand-Alone, Open-Source StarCraft II Benchmark for Accessible Reinforcement Learning Research

Sourav Panda1   Shreyash Kale1   Tanmay Ambadkar1   Abhinav Verma1   Jonathan Dodge1

1The Pennsylvania State University

Abstract

Reinforcement learning research in StarCraft II often sits between two extremes: highly simplified mini-games and computationally expensive full-game pipelines. This project introduces the Two-Bridge Benchmark as a stand-alone, open-source middle ground designed for accessible and reproducible experimentation. The benchmark supports camera-free and camera-lock variants, Base/Navigate/Combat task families, and V1-V3 difficulty scaling, with PySC2 + Gymnasium wrappers and Stable-Baselines3 baselines for consistent evaluation.

Overview

The Two-Bridge Benchmark is a custom StarCraft II benchmark designed to support accessible, reproducible reinforcement learning research in a richer environment than typical micro mini-games.

It bridges the gap between overly simplified testbeds and full-game SC2 complexity through structured map variants, transparent trigger logic, and consistent evaluation pathways. The benchmark emphasizes reproducibility through explicit map assets, task variants, and baseline implementations.

Benchmark at a Glance

Map Families / Variants

Camera-free and camera-lock map families with scenario-specific variants.

Objectives

Base, Navigate, and Combat tasks covering complementary control challenges.

Difficulty Versions

V1, V2, and V3 progression for curriculum-style scaling and stress tests.

Baselines

A2C, PPO, and Maskable PPO with Stable-Baselines3-compatible workflows.

Environment Wrappers

PySC2 and Gymnasium wrappers for standardized interfaces and reproducible rollouts.

Artifacts

Pretrained checkpoints, TensorBoard logs, replays, and evaluation outputs.
